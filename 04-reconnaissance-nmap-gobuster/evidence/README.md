# Evidence handling — Nmap & Gobuster

**Status: No evidence files uploaded yet.**

This folder is reserved for sanitized screenshots or terminal output from the **local-only**, authorized lab.

Before publishing:
1. Ensure the scan target is `127.0.0.1`; do **not** include your workplace, home-network public IP, third-party domains, real customer systems, tokens, credentials or identifiable device names.
2. If your terminal output contains your local account name, host name, personal folders or other sensitive information, redact those sections before uploading.
3. Keep filenames simple and traceable, such as `E01_nmap_port_scan.png`, `E02_nmap_service_detection.png`, `E03_gobuster_directory_results.png`.
4. Include a short explanation for every image in the report and confirm that the screenshot reflects a scan actually run.
5. Do not upload raw capture files or unredacted HTTP credentials from other sessions.

Do not use sample or fabricated Nmap/Gobuster output as if it were real evidence. Once testing is complete, update `lab-report-template.md` with verified findings and switch the project status to **Completed**.
